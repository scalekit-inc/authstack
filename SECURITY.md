# Security policy

## Supported versions

Security fixes go to the latest release of each kit, AgentKit and SaaSKit. Older releases are not patched.

## Report a vulnerability

Report it privately. Do not open a public issue or pull request.

1. Use GitHub's private report form: [Report a vulnerability](https://github.com/scalekit-inc/authstack/security/advisories/new).
2. Or email [security@scalekit.com](mailto:security@scalekit.com).

Include:

- The plugin or skill affected, and its version.
- Steps to reproduce.
- The impact you expect.

## What to expect

- We acknowledge your report within 3 business days.
- We tell you what we plan to do and keep you updated until it is fixed.
- We credit you in the fix when you want that.

## Scope

This policy covers the skills, plugin manifests, and scripts in this repository.

For a problem in the Scalekit service itself, such as the API, dashboard, or `mcp.scalekit.com`, use the same email address.
